## 1. Introduction
OhhBuddha ("we", "our", or "us") respects and values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application **OhhBuddha** available on the Google Play Store.

## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **No Account Required:** You can use OhhBuddha freely without creating an account or providing personal credentials (such as your name, email address, or phone number).
- **No Cloud Chat Storage:** Your conversations with the Dhamma AI assistant are processed locally on your device or ephemerally during AI responses. We do not store or sell personal chat logs to third parties.

### 2.2 Device & Hardware Permissions
To deliver specific features, OhhBuddha may request the following device permissions:
- **Audio / Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used solely for real-time speech-to-text input when you speak questions into the AI prompt. Audio recordings are never retained or saved on external servers.
- **Location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`):** Used solely on your device to calculate accurate local sunrise/sunset times and regional Buddhist lunar dates (Wan Phra) for your province. We do not track or log your location history.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to send scheduled Buddhist Holy Day (วันพระ) reminders at your chosen alert time.
- **Internet (`INTERNET`):** Used to communicate with AI model endpoints, retrieve Dhamma knowledge updates, and check for Google Play In-App Updates.

## 3. Data Safety & Security
- We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for network transmissions.
- Your bookmarks, customized font sizes, and alert preferences are stored locally in your device's private application storage (`SharedPreferences` / local Room database).
- We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to any advertisers or marketing agencies.

## 4. Google Play In-App Updates
OhhBuddha integrates the official **Google Play In-App Update API** provided by Google LLC. This allows the application to check Google Play servers directly for newer versions and facilitate seamless updates. All update delivery complies with Google Play's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 5. Third-Party Services
Our application does not include third-party advertising SDKs or tracking pixels. If external links (such as TikTok or Facebook fanpages) are accessed, their respective privacy policies apply.

## 6. Children's Privacy
OhhBuddha does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The application is designed to be safe and suitable for users of all ages interested in Buddhist teachings and mindfulness.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
